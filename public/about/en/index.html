What ParkinON does

ParkinON helps people with Parkinson's disease and the family members who care for them take medication on time and record health together. Large text and simple buttons keep it easy to use, with no complicated setup.

Main features

💊

Dose reminders

Reminders at the times you set — morning, lunch, dinner, bedtime and more.

📋

Condition tracking

Log how you feel after each dose — condition, mood and sleep — and watch how it changes.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family link

Connect with family using an invite code and follow the patient's records together.

🏃

Exercise & info

Exercise guidance and information helpful for living with Parkinson's.
