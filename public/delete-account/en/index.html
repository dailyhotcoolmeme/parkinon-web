Account & Data Deletion

ParkinON

This page explains how to delete your account and data in the ParkinON app.
App: ParkinON
Developer: ourmine

Option 1 — Delete your account in the app (recommended)

ParkinON lets you delete your account directly in the app. Follow the steps below and your account and all personal data are permanently deleted right away.

  1. Open the ParkinON app.
  2. Go to the My Info tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and choose "Delete account".
  4. Read the notice and confirm the deletion.
  5. Your account and all personal data are permanently deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot use or access the app, you can request account deletion by email.

  • Support email: admin@ourmine.co.kr
  • Please use "ParkinON account deletion request" as the subject.
  • Include the email (social sign-in account) you used to sign up so we can verify it is you.

Once we receive your request, we verify your identity, delete your account and all personal data without delay, and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, all of the following personal data is permanently removed.

  • Account information — name, email, profile image, linked social account, birth year, gender, role
  • Health records — medication logs, condition/mood/sleep/constipation records, exercise logs, symptom notes, motion measurements, visit records, appointments, diary entries
  • Media — photos, videos and voice recordings you uploaded (including family voice alarm sounds)
  • Community data — posts and comments you wrote
  • Family link data — patient–caregiver links and link history
  • Any other personal data created while using the service

Data retention

When your account is deleted, all personal data above is permanently erased immediately and cannot be recovered.

Where Korean law (such as the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce or the Protection of Communications Secrets Act) requires certain records to be kept, those items are stored separately for the period required by that law and then destroyed. Even then they are never used for any purpose other than the legal retention requirement.

Item When deleted / retention period
Account info, health records, visit records & appointments, motion measurements, diary, photos/videos/voice, posts & comments, family link data Permanently deleted immediately on account deletion
Service usage logs & access IP Destroyed within 3 months
Items with a legal retention requirement Stored separately for the period required by law, then destroyed

See the Privacy Policy for details on what is collected and stored.
