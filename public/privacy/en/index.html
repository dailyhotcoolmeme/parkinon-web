Article 1-2 (Restriction on Sign-Up by Children Under 14)

This Service is intended for people with Parkinson’s and their care partners aged 14 or older. The Company restricts sign-up by children under 14 by verifying birth year at registration, and does not collect personal information from children under 14. If it is confirmed that a child under 14 has registered, the Company will delete the relevant account and personal information without delay.

Article 4 (Provision to Third Parties)

The Company does not, in principle, provide Users' personal information to external parties. However, the following are exceptions:

  • Where the User has given prior consent
  • Where required by law, or requested by an investigative agency in accordance with legally prescribed procedures for investigative purposes

In addition, because this Service is designed for linked care between a patient and their care partner, a care partner and patient linked within the same family group may mutually view and share each other's health records (medication, body state, exercise, medical visits, motion measurements, etc.) and enter records on each other's behalf. This is an inherent function of the family-linking feature that Users request directly, and is provided for the purpose of delivering the Service — it does not constitute provision of personal information to an external third party. Disabling family linking stops this mutual access, sharing, and proxy input.

Article 6 (Processing of Sensitive Information)

The Company collects sensitive personal information such as health status and medication records. This information is used solely for providing the Service and is encrypted and stored securely.

To provide automatic recognition (OCR) of prescription/medical-record photos, the Company transmits the relevant images to Anthropic PBC (United States). Transmitted images are used solely for OCR analysis and are not stored on any server, database, file storage (R2), or device — they are destroyed immediately after analysis. Of the analysis results, personally identifying information such as the patient's name is not stored; only medication information (drug name, dosage, time of administration, etc.) is stored.

Article 6-2 (Separate Consent for Processing Sensitive Information)

In accordance with Article 23 of the Personal Information Protection Act, the Company processes sensitive information such as health-related data separately from other personal information, and only after obtaining separate consent from the data subject.

Item Processed Purpose of Processing Retention Period
Parkinson's diagnosis information (diagnosis year, etc.), medication records, symptom/body-state/mood/sleep/constipation records, medical visit records and appointments, motion measurement values (digital biomarkers) Providing the Service, including medication management, symptom/medication-effect tracking, appointment management, and family-linked care Until account withdrawal (deleted immediately upon withdrawal)

The above sensitive information is used solely for the purpose of providing the Service and is encrypted and stored securely. Users may decline consent to the processing of sensitive information; however, doing so will restrict use of ParkinON's core features.

The Service may automatically collect device information (OS, app version), service usage records, and access IP addresses for app operation purposes. Article 9-2 (Advertising Identifier and Non-Personalized Ads)

The Service may display banner advertisements from third-party advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob) to Users on the free plan. Advertisements are served on a non-personalized basis by default, and the Company does not provide any health-related data (medication, symptom, or activity records) to advertising SDKs for targeting purposes. Users may reset or limit their device's advertising identifier through their device's operating-system settings. Subscribing to the Premium Plan removes in-app advertisements.
